Diplomatic backdrop · Why this matters now

In early 2026, Germany and Canada launched a Sovereign Technology Alliance on the sidelines of the Munich Security Conference to reduce strategic technology dependencies and build sovereign AI capabilities. In April 2026, Toronto-based Cohere merged with Heidelberg-based Aleph Alpha in a $20B transatlantic deal anchored by Germany's Schwarz Group — making ALL IN 2026 the natural stage to amplify this corridor. The shortlist below is built to advance that corridor and showcase the depth of European industrial AI to a Canadian audience.
